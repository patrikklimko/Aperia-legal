Common questions

  • Managing your subscription: Aperia Plus is billed through the Apple App Store. You can view, change, or cancel it anytime in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.
  • Restoring a purchase: Open Aperia → Subscription → Restore purchase, while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.
  • Your data & privacy: You can export or permanently delete your data from within the app. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Terms: Review our Terms of Use.
